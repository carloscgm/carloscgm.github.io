Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Leve is an app for reviewing the photos and videos on your phone and freeing up space. It is built so your photos never leave your device. This page explains what data it uses and what it does with it.

  • Leve doesn't collect or send any personal data. There's no account, no ads and no analytics.
  • Your photos and videos are read on your phone and never leave it.
  • Whatever the app stores stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall it.

What the app reads

With your permission, Leve reads the photos and videos in your gallery and their basic details: size, date, dimensions, length, file name, album and whether they're favorites. It uses them only to show them to you, work out how much space you can free and remember which ones you've reviewed. If you give access to only some photos, Leve only sees those.

On Android it also reads the total and free storage, to show you how much you've freed.

What it stores, and where

Inside the app, on your phone, Leve stores the list of photos and videos with those details, what you decide for each one (keep, trash or later), your progress (daily goal, streak and achievements) and your settings. None of this leaves the device or is shared with anyone.

It's removed when you uninstall the app, and you can reset your progress in Settings. If you have system backups turned on (Google or iCloud), the system may include this data in them, as with any other app. Leve has no access to those backups.

What Leve doesn't do

There's no account or sign-in, no ads, and no analytics, tracking or third-party tools that collect data. On Android it doesn't even have permission to connect to the internet. It doesn't sell or share data with anyone, because it has none.

Deleting photos

Leve only deletes what you send to its trash and then confirm, and always through the system's own confirmation dialog.

On Android, photos and videos are deleted from the phone. Any copies in Google Photos or another cloud stay there.

On iPhone and iPad they go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where they're kept for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, they're also deleted from iCloud and your other devices.

iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos with “Optimize Storage”, iOS may download from iCloud the photos that aren't on the device so Leve can show them to you. That download is done by Apple's system, not by Leve, and nothing new is sent to anyone.

Reminders

If a few days go by without opening Leve, the app may show you up to four notifications about what's left to review or free. They're prepared and shown on your phone, from the data already on it: they don't go through any server and send nothing. They only contain numbers, never photos or file names. You can turn them off in Leve's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Ratings

Now and then, Leve may show the Google Play or App Store rating dialog. That dialog belongs to the store: Leve doesn't know whether you saw it or what you wrote. Google or Apple handle what you write under their own privacy policies.

Children

Leve isn't aimed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.
