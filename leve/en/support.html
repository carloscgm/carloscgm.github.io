Help

Answers to the most common questions about Leve. If yours isn't here, write to [CORREO].

Where are the photos I deleted?

Android

Leve deletes them from the phone through Android's dialog, and they don't go to any system trash, so they can't be recovered from the phone. If you had a copy in Google Photos or another cloud, that copy is still there: look for it in your cloud app.

iPhone and iPad

They're in Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days, and you can recover them from there. If you use iCloud Photos, they were also deleted from your other devices and are recovered from the same album.

Is anything deleted while I swipe?

No. Swiping left only sends the photo to Leve's trash: it stays in your gallery. You can undo your last swipes and rescue any photo from the trash.

Things are only deleted when you tap “Free up” in the trash, confirm it in Leve and once more in the system dialog.

I deleted photos on my iPhone but have no more space

iOS keeps deleted items for 30 days in Recently Deleted, in Photos. Empty that album to get the space now; otherwise it frees up on its own after those days.

Why does it ask for access to all my photos?

To show you your whole gallery, one item at a time, work out how much space each photo takes and remember which ones you've reviewed. If you give access to only some, Leve works, but only with those. You can change it any time in your phone's settings.

Does it need the internet?

No. Leve works offline. On iPhone, if you use iCloud Photos, iOS may need the internet to bring in photos that aren't on the device.

Does it cost anything?

No. Leve is free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

What happens to my progress if I uninstall the app?

It goes with the app: what you've reviewed, Leve's trash, your streak and your achievements. Your photos aren't touched. If you reinstall, you start from scratch, unless the system restores a backup.

How do I see the guide again or start over?

In Settings: “Show the guide again” and “Reset progress”. Resetting puts every photo back in the queue and deletes none.

What does Leve do with my data?

Nothing outside your phone: it doesn't collect or send any data. The privacy policy explains it.
